# LinkedIn

## Hard limits

- **3,000 characters** in the post body. Assista refuses anything longer.
- Text, one image, or 2 to 20 images. No document (PDF) carousels: a carousel
  ships as several images.
- **No link in the body.** LinkedIn downranks posts with an external link, and
  Assista's checks refuse it. If the link matters, say "link in the first
  comment" and tell the user to add it there.

## What the feed shows

- Only the first two or three lines show before "…see more", roughly the
  first 200 characters. The first line has to earn the click on its own. Do
  not spend it on "I've been thinking about…".
- Line breaks render as written. Use short paragraphs (one to three sentences)
  with a blank line between them. Do not put every sentence on its own line:
  that is "broetry" and gets flagged.

## Norms

- First person, professional but not stiff. A specific story beats a lesson.
- 150 to 300 words is the usual sweet spot. Shorter is fine when the idea is
  small. Do not pad to fill the limit.
- Hashtags: zero to three, at the very end. Many strong writers use none.
- Mentions (`@Name`) do not resolve from plain text. Write the person's name
  without the `@`.
- End on a real point or a genuine question, not "Agree?" or "Thoughts?".
