# X

## Hard limits

- **280 characters**, including spaces and line breaks. Premium accounts can
  post longer, but Assista cannot tell which tier the writer has, so it
  enforces 280. Count before you submit.
- **No link in the body, ever.** A post with a URL costs 13 times as much to
  publish, so Assista refuses it. Put the link in a reply and tell the user.
- Text, or up to four images.

## Writing for it

- One idea per post. If it needs three paragraphs, it is a LinkedIn post, or
  a thread the writer has to post themselves (Assista publishes single posts).
- Lead with the point. There is no "see more" to hide a slow start behind.
- Cut filler words before you cut the specific detail. The number, the name or
  the date is the reason the post exists.
- Line breaks are fine and count as characters. One or two at most.

## Norms

- Hashtags: zero or one. More reads as spam on X.
- Plain, conversational, a little sharper than LinkedIn. No corporate tone.
- No "🧵" or "1/" markers: Assista posts one post, not a thread.
