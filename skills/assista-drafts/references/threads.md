# Threads

## Hard limits

- **500 characters** in the post body.
- Text, one image, or a carousel of 2 to 20 images.
- Links are allowed and become clickable, but Assista's checks still refuse a
  URL in the body. Put it in a reply, or drop it.

## Writing for it

- Casual and conversational: closer to talking than to a LinkedIn post.
- One idea, two or three short paragraphs at most. Line breaks render as
  written.
- Start with the interesting part. The feed moves fast.

## Norms

- Hashtags: Threads links only one topic tag per post. Use zero or one, at
  the end.
- Questions that invite a real reply do well. "What do you think?" does not
  count as one.
- No cross-post tone. A LinkedIn post pasted here reads as a press release.
