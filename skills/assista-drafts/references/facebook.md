# Facebook

## Hard limits

- **63,206 characters** in the post body. In practice, never come close.
- Assista publishes to a Facebook **Page**, not a personal profile.
- Text, one image, or several images.
- Links are allowed and become clickable, but Assista's checks still refuse a
  URL in the body. Put it in a comment, or drop it.

## Writing for it

- Only the first few lines show before "See more", roughly the first 125
  characters on mobile. Open with the point.
- Shorter posts do better: about 40 to 80 words is a good default. Go longer
  only for a real story.
- Short paragraphs with blank lines between them. Line breaks render as
  written.

## Norms

- Warm and direct. The audience is often customers and locals rather than
  peers, so explain jargon or drop it.
- Hashtags: zero to two. They do little on Facebook.
- Address the reader as "you". A clear next step ("reply with…", "come by on
  Friday") beats a vague question.
